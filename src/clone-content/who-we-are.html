About Us

Our staff & volunteers

We are a small group dedicated to helping cats and kittens in southern Glenn County.  All of us live in Glenn County and foster kitties as well as have our own beloved fur babies.  More to come....

Our history

Catnip and Cattitude is a small 501c3 non-profit organization that was formed in September 2021. We are focusing on spaying and neutering (altering) cats and kittens that are in need in Southern Glenn County. 

Our mission

 

We plan to work with the colonies in Southern Glenn County with the TNR (Trap Spay/Neuter Return) program to help with the health of the existing populations. This is done by altering ferals and vaccinating them for rabies. They are then released back into their original location where they are cared for by the locals in that area.

We are committed to helping the abandoned, forgotten, and surrendered cats and kittens. We will continue to spay, neuter, vaccinate and place friendly kitties in loving forever homes for as long as there is a need.
